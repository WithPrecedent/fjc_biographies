1"""Default settings for downloading and parsing the FJC's judge biographies. 

2 

3These are module-level constants so that a project built on `fjc_biographies` 

4can change them before they are used (for example, 

5`fjc_biographies.options._TIMEOUT = 600` to wait longer for the FJC website). 

6 

7Contents: 

8 _ABA_RATINGS: the number for each rating of the American Bar Association. 

9 _APPEALS_CIRCUITS: the number of each court of appeals' circuit, by the 

10 words that name it. 

11 _CATEGORIES: the FJC's files of the directory, by what `fjc_biographies` 

12 calls each. 

13 _COLUMN_NAMES: names for the columns whose names are not made the usual 

14 way (see `parser.tidy`). 

15 _DATA_FOLDER: the data folder when fjc_biographies runs from its 

16 repository. 

17 _DISTRICT_CIRCUITS: the number of the circuit of each state or territory 

18 with a district court. 

19 _ENV_DATA: environment variable that can name the data folder. 

20 _EXPORT_URL: address of the page that links to the FJC's files. 

21 _FJC_URL: address of the folder with the FJC's files. 

22 _PARTIES: the number for each party of an appointing president. 

23 _TIMEOUT: seconds to wait for the FJC website before giving up. 

24 _USER_AGENT: how fjc_biographies identifies itself to servers. 

25 

26"""
