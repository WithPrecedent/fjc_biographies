1"""Rules that make new fields from the text of judges' biographies. 

2 

3The rules are written in CSV files (spreadsheets), so they can be read and 

4changed without knowing Python. They are the rules of courtpy, whose "judges" 

5rulebook (in fjc_biographies' "instructions" folder) codes each judge's 

6professional career. Each row is one rule. The columns are: 

7 

8| Column | Meaning | 

9| --- | --- | 

10| `target` | The section of text to search, such as "career". Several sections can be listed, separated by commas, and are searched in order. | 

11| `variable` | The name of the column that the rule makes (or adds to). | 

12| `kind` | What the rule does (see below). | 

13| `pattern` | A regular expression to search for. | 

14| `value` | For a `label` rule, the value to record. Otherwise blank. | 

15| `ignorecase` | TRUE to ignore the difference between capital and lower-case letters. | 

16| `dotall` | TRUE for "." in the pattern to also match line breaks. | 

17| `note` | Anything you want to say about the rule. It is not used. | 

18 

19The kinds of rules: 

20 

21| Kind | Makes | 

22| --- | --- | 

23| `flag` | TRUE if the pattern appears, and otherwise FALSE. | 

24| `count` | The number of times the pattern appears. | 

25| `matches` | A list of every match. | 

26| `label` | The rule's `value` if the pattern appears. The first rule for a variable that matches wins, so it can turn text into categories (such as kinds of positions into numbers). | 

27| `names` | A list of names, found by splitting the text wherever the pattern matches. The pattern should match the words and punctuation between names. | 

28| `section` | A new section of text: the first match. Later rules can search it. | 

29| `excerpts` | A new section of text: every match, one per line. | 

30| `split` | Two new sections, named in `variable` (such as "before, after"): the text before and after the first match. | 

31| `remove` | Nothing. It deletes every match from the target for the rules after it. | 

32 

33Rules are applied from top to bottom, so a rule can search a section made by 

34a rule above it. Rules with the same `variable` are combined: flags are TRUE if 

35any rule matches, counts are added, lists of matches and names are joined, and 

36the first `label` or `section` that matches is used. 

37 

38Contents: 

39 KINDS: the kinds of rules. 

40 Rule: one rule from a CSV file. 

41 Rulebook: an ordered collection of rules. 

42 find_instructions: returns the CSV files for a built-in or custom rulebook. 

43 split_names: splits text into names wherever a pattern matches. 

44
